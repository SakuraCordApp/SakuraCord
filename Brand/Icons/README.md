# SakuraCord icon variants

These PNG exports provide ready-to-use icon shapes without replacing the
editable Icon Composer projects in `App/Packaging`.

## Apple App Icon

- Default, Dark, and flower-only Apple-rendered app icons.
- Transparent outer corners preserve the native Apple icon silhouette.
- Sizes: 1024, 512, 256, 128, 64, 32, and 16 pixels.

## Circle

- Full SakuraCord and flower-only marks on a circular SakuraCord gradient.
- The area outside the circle is transparent.
- Sizes: 1024, 512, 256, 128, 64, and 32 pixels.

## Rounded Square

- Full SakuraCord and flower-only marks on a generic rounded-square SakuraCord
  gradient.
- The area outside the rounded square is transparent.
- Sizes: 1024, 512, 256, 128, 64, and 32 pixels.

## Rounded Rectangle

- Full SakuraCord and flower-only marks on a 2:1 rounded-rectangle SakuraCord
  gradient.
- The area outside the rounded rectangle is transparent.
- Sizes: 1200x600, 1000x500, 800x400, 600x300, 400x200, and 200x100 pixels.

The glass effects in the marks come from the native 1024-pixel renders. Smaller
files are downsampled from those masters.
